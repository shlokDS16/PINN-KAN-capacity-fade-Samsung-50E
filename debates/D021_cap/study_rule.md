## Facts that fix the candidate set (read from code, not assumed)
- `N_COEFS` = zero 0, linear 2, log 2, quadratic 3, power 3, exponential 3, arrhenius 3, hinge 3. `choose_family` excludes a family iff `N_COEFS[f] > cap`.
- So the cap is a three-way switch: cap <= 1 leaves only `zero` (every edge pruned); cap = 2 leaves {zero, linear, log}; cap >= 3 leaves the whole library. Cap 4 and above are the same extraction as cap 3 by construction.
- The candidate set is therefore {2, 3}, with cap 4 as an equivalence check and cap 1 as a degenerate check. "One neighbour on each side" of cap 3 is cap 2 (real) and cap 4 (identical by construction, verified below); of cap 2 it is cap 1 (degenerate) and cap 3.

## Study design (fixed now)
Data: `synth.make_synthetic(kind, n_cyc_cond=40, n_sto_cond=8, reps=2, seed)` for kind in {G0 (additive truth), G2 (non-additive truth with a hinge)}; the 3 cycling temperatures of the design are the 3 levels of the real folds. 8 conditions held out (`split_conditions(.., 0.2, seed)`), 32 conditions train (m = 32); m = 8 by the project's own max-min selector `data.apply_subset` (>= 2 conditions per temperature). Seeds 0, 1, 2, ... in seed-major order; the number of complete seed groups is whatever finishes inside the time limit (<= 50 min wall, <= 2 processes).
Arms: A15 with h = 1, A15 with h = 2, G15 (the project's `arms.SymbolicArm` steps: parent `fit_pinn`, `extract_law`, refit with `refit_frac` 0.5), hyperparameters fixed at the grid middles `arms.default_hp` (no tuning; identical for every cap), steps_scale 1.0, model seed = design seed. Caps extracted: 2, 3, 4 (cap 4: extraction only, compared with cap 3); refit and scored: 2 and 3. Cap 1 and the per-family edge study are run separately (`d021_edge_study.py`).
Metrics (all label-free of Samsung; truth known):
- E_law = centred RMS error of the fitted cyclic log-rate surface against the truth `SynthSet.log_rate_cyc`, divided by the range of the truth, on a probe set of 300 points (Latin hypercube over the training-hull box of soc_mid, dod, c_ch, c_dch, at the 3 temperature levels). The parent KAN is scored the same way (reference). A probe at the 8 held-out conditions' stress vectors gives E_test (same normalisation).
- Forecast RMSE (pp) of the refitted model on the held-out cycling cells (window k = 3; 60 inversion steps).
- n_coefs; the family chosen on every first-layer edge; for G0, "false structure" = a 3-coefficient family chosen on an input whose truth is linear (T, c_ch, c_dch, dod) and "false prune" = zero chosen on such an input (A15 h = 1 and G15 only, where an edge is an input's function).
- A15/G15 symmetry: on G0, paired difference E_law(A15 h=1) - E_law(G15) and its change with the cap.

## Decision rule (applied by `d021_cap_decide.py` to the output files; nobody chooses afterwards)
D0 Equivalence. Cap 4 extraction equals cap 3 (family and theta, every edge, every fit). If not, stop: the reading of the cap is wrong; report to the lead.
D1 Admissibility by requirement (not by data). The plan needs a 3-coefficient family on at least one edge: D016 section 3.5 (does the calendar SOC slope change sign above SOC 0.8: needs quadratic or hinge on the calendar SOC edge) and PREREG 7.3 item 4 / the frozen unit tests (each library family recovered). So cap 3 is the smallest cap that keeps the pre-registered analyses possible. Cap 2 is admissible only if D2 shows that cap 3 does harm.
D2 Harm test for cap 3 (primary cell m = 32). For kind in {G0, G2}: d(r) = mean over the three arms of E_law(cap 3) - E_law(cap 2) for replicate r. Cap 3 is harmful in that kind iff the one-sided 95 % upper bound (percentile bootstrap over replicate seeds, 10,000 draws, rng seed 20261105) of mean d exceeds +0.02 (twice the library's own 1 % resolution `rms_tol_rel`).
D3 Verdict. Cap 3 if cap 3 is harmful in neither kind. Cap 2 if cap 3 is harmful in at least one kind; in that case the lead is told that D016 3.5 (sign change of the SOC slope) and the seven-family recovery test must be dropped or rewritten, because cap 2 cannot support them.
D4 Guards that do not change the verdict, reported with it: (a) m = 8 harm bound of cap 3 against cap 2 (a bound above +0.05 is printed as a limitation of the descriptive cell g1; K1 and K2 are scored at g4 only); (b) A15/G15 symmetry on G0 at the chosen cap: |mean(E_law A15h1 - E_law G15)| <= 0.02, else flagged; (c) false-structure and false-prune rates on G0 at the chosen cap, with the temperature edge reported separately (3 levels).
What would re-open the cap: the verdict flips if the unit-of-analysis changes (a different library), if the real folds have fewer than 3 temperature levels in training for a scored arm (Test C: 2 levels), or if a replicate shows cap 4 != cap 3.
