# D022 extraction-audit calibration: rule (written and committed BEFORE the calibration code `code/prereg/extraction_calibration.py` is written or run)

Author: coder (build_v2), 2026-11-05, from D022 judge section 2.2 (a) items 2, 3 and 5 (`debates/D022_datasets_kan/03_judge.md`). Reading: full text of that section and of Actions A2 and A4; `code/pinnkan/symbolic.py` (cap 2 in `symbolic_library.json`), `synth.py`, `runs/prereg/subsets.json` (identifiers only). No Samsung label, outer-test datum or score was opened. Nothing here was run. The thresholds are in `code/pinnkan/extraction_audit.json`; this file fixes the calibration design. Both files are in the frozen commit and their sha256 values are config keys (`extraction_audit_sha256`, `calibration_rule_sha256`). Any number below is a design choice, not a result.

## 1. Unit and cells
- **Edge** = one input of one extracted law (T, SOC_mid, DoD, C_ch, C_dch). A *cell* of the calibration is (input, m, arm, truth family). Arms: `A15h1` (A15 with h = 1), `A15h2` (A15 with h = 2), `G15`. The pipeline is the project's own `arms.SymbolicArm` (parent `fit_pinn`, extraction at the library cap, end-to-end refit with the matched budget), default hyperparameters `arms.default_hp` apart from `h`, `steps_scale` 1.0, backbone `self_limiting`, no tuning (as in D021).
- m in {32, 8}. The training design of a synthetic dataset is the real one: for outer fold f in 0..4 and each m, the fold's training cycling conditions are the first m of `subsets.json` (identifiers only, nested order) and the storage conditions are the 19 storage conditions of the S2 split (identifiers only); two replicate cells per condition, one per storage condition (PC-1 assumptions of PREREG 13). Retention values are generated by the synthetic truth; no Samsung retention is read.

## 2. Datasets and the fixed assignment table
- **Additive truth blocks (the label-bearing cells).** S = 12 datasets per (fold, m). Dataset j in 0..11 draws, for input i in 0..4, the truth family `FAM[(j + i) mod 3]` with `FAM = (zero, linear, log)` and the slope sign `+1` if `(j + i)` is even, else `-1` (zero has no sign). Over the 12 datasets every input meets each truth family exactly 4 times; over the 5 folds that is **20 synthetic edges per (input, m, arm, truth family)**, the minimum required (judge 2.2a item 2).
- **Truth functions** (log-rate contribution of input i, amplitude A = 0.5, x_i the physical stress value, [lo_i, hi_i] the training-hull range of that input in the fold at that m): zero: 0; linear: `sign * A * (2 (x - lo)/(hi - lo) - 1)`; log: `sign * A * (2 log(x/lo)/log(hi/lo) - 1)` (T in kelvin, other inputs on their physical positive scale). The law level, calendar term, noise (0.5 pp), per-cell offset sd 0.15 and the median final fade of 20 pp follow `synth.make_synthetic` (PREREG 13).
- **Report-only curved block.** 6 datasets per (fold, m): input i gets `quadratic` (`A (2 u^2 - 1)`) or `hinge` (`A (2 max(0, u - 0.2)/0.8 - 1)`, with u the hull-scaled coordinate in [-1, 1]) by `CURVE[(j + i) mod 2]`, `CURVE = (quadratic, hinge)`. These rows give representation error and the family chosen; they never enter a label rule.
- **Non-additive block (composition of A15 h = 2).** 6 datasets per (fold, m) of the project's non-additive kind G2 on the same design, arm `A15h2` only; reported as the share of laws whose outer edges are all linear/zero (the additivity condition of the label rule) and the families chosen.
- **Seeds.** Dataset seed = `SeedSequence([20261105, crc32("calib|<block>|f<fold>|m<m>|j<j>")]).generate_state(1)[0]`; the parent fit seed equals the dataset index j.
- **Cost** (design arithmetic, not a measurement): additive 5 x 2 x 3 x 12 = 360 pipeline fits, curved 5 x 2 x 3 x 6 = 180, non-additive 5 x 2 x 6 = 60; 600 fits in total.

## 3. What is recorded and the outputs
- Per (fold, m, arm, dataset, input): truth family, truth sign, chosen first-layer family (the input's family: `zero` if every first-layer edge of the input is zero, the family of its single non-zero edge, else `composed`), the sign of the extracted effect (finite difference of the extracted cyclic map over the input's hull with the other inputs at the hull midpoint), BIC margin of the dominant edge, outer-edge families, and the representation error of the extracted effect against the truth effect (centred RMS over the hull / range of the truth effect).
- Output tables per (input, m, arm, truth family): confusion counts of the chosen family and of the sign, with exact (Clopper-Pearson) binomial 95 % intervals (descriptive). The calibrated *false-family rate* of an eligible family F in a cell (input, m, arm) is `sum over truth families T != F, T in {zero, linear, log}, of count(T -> F) / sum over the same T of n(T)`; the *sign-error rate* is the share of nonzero-truth edges (truth linear or log) whose extracted sign differs from the truth sign. A cell counts only if every truth family has n >= 20.
- Files (written by the script under `runs/extraction_calibration/`): `calibration.json` (tables, rates, the rule hash, the thresholds hash, the FROZEN commit), `records.jsonl` (one line per edge), `calibration_hash.json` (sha256 and write time, same mechanism as item (f)).

## 4. Order and guard
1. This rule file is committed before the script. 2. The script refuses to run unless `guard.verify_freeze` passes (HEAD equals the frozen commit and the tree is clean), the thresholds file hash and this file's hash equal the config pins, and no outer score exists (`runs/prereg/SCORED.jsonl` absent or empty). 3. The statistics step refuses to print edge-level law wording unless `calibration_hash.json` exists, matches `calibration.json`, and was written before the first outer score file.

## 5. What would void the calibration
A change to this file, to the thresholds file or to the extraction path (`symbolic.py`, `symbolic_library.json`) after the calibration run; a calibration run from a commit other than the FROZEN one; a Samsung outer score computed before `calibration_hash.json` is written.
