# Two further 2026 JES papers read from arXiv (lower topical priority)

## A. Neural posterior estimation for inverse parameter inference (Hassanaly, Randall, Weddle, Gasper et al.)
**Citation.** JES 179 (issue 2026-11), article 123823, "Neural posterior estimation for scalable and accurate inverse parameter inference in Li-ion batteries", DOI 10.1016/j.est.2026.123823 (verified on Crossref); arXiv 2604.02520 (CC-BY). Affiliations not checked.
**Reading depth: arXiv v1 HTML, headings, contribution list and all captions read; body paragraphs not read line by line; figures not viewed.** Files `refs/exemplars/md/arxiv_2604.02520.txt`.
- Claims: **5 numbered contributions** (NPE as accurate as Bayesian calibration at ms cost on synthetic data; interpretability via local sensitivity; experimental aging modes LLI and LAM recovered; physics-based constraints via the prior; tractable up to 27 parameters). Sections 1 Introduction (1.1 Motivation, 1.2 Related work, 1.3 Contributions), 2 Methods, 3 Results (3.1 comparison with Bayesian calibration, 3.2 experimental data, 3.3 high-dimensional tractability), 4 Conclusions, Appendices A-F (parameters, solver details, hyper-parameter search spaces, noise sensitivity, priors).
- 11 figures, 9 tables (including 5 appendix tables). Forms (captions): architecture schematic (CNN/FCNN blocks) for estimator and surrogate; train/test loss histories; **posterior overlays: near-match and mismatch examples side by side** (honest failure case); Wasserstein distance table; conditional relative-error plots per parameter; absolute SHAP heat plots on voltage curve; LLI and LAM measured vs predicted mean and standard deviation along cycle number (Figs 9-10, with and without Li conservation in the prior); learning-curve plot of metric loss vs training size for 6-, 16-, 27-dimensional cases with a linear trend. Tables: accuracy vs Bayesian calibration "best in bold", with the **noise-model sensitivity repeated in the appendix** (Tables 7-8).
- Use for us: showing a failure/mismatch example next to a success; noise-sensitivity table in the appendix; hyper-parameter search spaces in appendix tables (answers "how was it tuned" in two tables).

## B. Delay-embedded dynamic mode decomposition (Labib and Ahmed)
**Citation.** JES 182 (issue 2026-12), article 124567, "Modeling of non-linear dynamics and degradation characterization of lithium-ion batteries using delay-embedded dynamic mode decomposition", DOI 10.1016/j.est.2026.124567 (verified on Crossref); arXiv 2601.22403.
**Reading depth: headings and captions only (short paper).**
- Sections I Introduction, II Background (Hankel matrix, DMD, DMDc), III Experimental set-up, IV Results (IV-A Hankel formation, IV-B identification and validation, IV-C **adaptability at degraded states**), V Conclusions. 5 figures. Forms: set-up schematic; RSS vs delay-embedding order; measured vs DMD/DMDc discharge profiles with zoomed time windows (b)-(d) at 1-2.5 h, 5-6.5 h, 10-12.5 h; model trained at cycle 20 predicting cycles 80 and 340; RSS across degradation levels.
- Use for us: "train on early cycle, predict later cycles" figure (Fig. 4) is the same logic as our window-then-forecast task; the zoomed time windows (b)-(d) show how a forecast with a long horizon is displayed in three zooms.
- Low priority: not a forecasting-of-capacity-fade paper, no stress-condition design.
