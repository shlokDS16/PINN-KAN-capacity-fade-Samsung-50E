## Problem and stated contribution
Five indirect health features (IHFs) from charge-discharge voltage and capacity-increment curves are refined by a CNN (convolution + pooling) and fed to a KAN for SOH. SHAP is used for feature contributions. Claims (abstract): RMSE < 0.007, MAE < 0.005, MAPE < 0.006 on CALCE, Oxford, TRI and MIT datasets; better than errors "in other studies".
