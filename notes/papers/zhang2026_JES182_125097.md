## Problem and stated contribution

Joint estimation of SOH and SOC-SOH-dependent parameters of a first-order RC (1RC) equivalent-circuit model, by a neural network wrapped around a differentiable 1RC voltage model (Eqs. 1-8, Fig. 5-6). Three stated contributions (lines 48-53): (1) the ECM-PINN framework; (2) comparison of NN-inverted parameters with conventional 1RC identification on Samsung 50E; (3) an SOH-guided parameter-consistency regularisation, evaluated on HUST dynamic discharge data under unseen-cell conditions. It is a voltage/impedance-model paper, not a capacity-fade forecasting paper (Section 1 positions it against ECM-parameter-identification work, Table 1 lines 39-45).

## Data

- **Samsung INR21700-50E** (ref [39], Stroebl et al., Sci. Data 11, 1020): NCA, 4.9 Ah, 3.6 V; "279 cells" in the full dataset (line 61).
- **Cells used**: 104 cycle-aged cells with complete cycling, capacity and HPPC records: 34 at 23 C, 35 at 35 C, 35 at 45 C. These give 162, 175, 195 diagnostic sequences (532 total). All HPPC diagnostics were run at 23 C. Three 45 C sequences dropped for missing SOC segments, leaving 529 (lines 62, 384). Calendar-ageing cells: not used. Which of the cycling conditions (SOC window, DoD, C-rates, laboratory) the 104 cells cover is not stated.
- **Input**: the 1C discharge pulses plus relaxation at nominal SOC 95, 90, 80, ..., 10, 5 % are cut from each HPPC record, resampled to 1 s and concatenated in descending SOC; polarisation state reset at each segment boundary; max length 693; tensor 529 x 693 x 6 (line 384). Six features: I, dI, V, dV, OCV minus V, SOC (line 147). Standardisation from training-set valid steps only (line 383).
- **Targets**: (i) SOH, supervised (Eq. 10); the SOH definition (capacity ratio against which reference) is not stated in the captured text; (ii) terminal voltage (reconstruction loss, Eq. 9); (iii) R0, R1, C1 at SOC nodes, validated against conventional Joint-L2 1RC identification from the same pulses (line 401), which serves as the reference, not as a physical ground truth.
- **Split**: by cell. Train 89 cells / 463 sequences; test 15 cells / 66 sequences (line 391, Table 2). "completely unseen during training" is the paper's phrase for the test cells. How the 15 test cells were chosen (random, stratified, seed) is not stated. No validation set is described for Samsung; no cross-validation.
- **HUST** (ref [40], Ma et al.): 77 cells reported, 76 used (cell 7-5 dropped), LFP 1.1 Ah, 30 C; 251 SOH levels per cell (0.75 to 1.00 at 0.001) gives 19,076 samples; 64 train / 12 test cells (lines 75, 390-391). Not our dataset; used here only to describe the paper.

## Model

- 1RC ECM: V = OCV(SOC) - I R0(SOC,SOH) - V1; V1 updated by the exact discrete exponential recursion (Eqs. 1-5). R0, R1, C1 are functions of SOC and SOH (Eqs. 6-8); in practice the network emits R0, R1, tau1 at predefined SOC nodes and C1 = tau1/R1 (line 148). OCV(SOC) is taken as given (its source is not described in the captured text).
- Network: CNN encoder, temporal compression, Transformer; a global feature Z (pooling) feeds an SOH head; a parameter head combines Z, a dynamic feature F and the predicted SOH (lines 147-148). Embedding 128, feed-forward 256, batch 64, up to 500 epochs, lr 3e-4 halved on a 10-epoch training-loss plateau; about 1.218 million trainable parameters (lines 398, 506). RTX 3090, PyTorch 2.3.1.
- Physics prior: the 1RC voltage recursion is inside the forward pass and enforced through the voltage reconstruction loss (a differentiable-model prior, not a PDE residual). Total loss, Eq. 14: weighted sum of voltage loss (Eq. 9), SOH loss (Eq. 10), a second-difference smoothness term along SOC (Eq. 11) and an SOH-guided consistency term over sample pairs (Eqs. 12-13).
- KAN: none. Physics-informed ageing law: none.

## What overlaps with our design, and what does not

Our design (D016 Design R2, `debates/D016_roles/02_judge.md` sections 1 and 3): a physics-informed neural calendar-plus-cycle capacity-fade law with Kolmogorov-Arnold stress functions on the Samsung 50E campaign, trained on all storage cells plus m of 32 cycling conditions, scored on 40 held-out cycling conditions (5 folds of 8), with a grid over m in {8, 16, 32} and check-ups k, matched against GAM, data-only and Stroebl-type semi-empirical arms, and a closed-form KAN law extracted and tested on held-out conditions.

- **Overlap (only this):** same cell (INR21700-50E), same public campaign (cited as ref [39]), neural network plus an embedded physical model, three ageing temperatures 23/35/45 C appear, and cell-level (not sequence-level) splitting is used to avoid leakage.
- **No overlap:** target (voltage, ECM parameters, SOH at the same diagnostic versus capacity-fade trajectory forecast); physical model (1RC circuit versus ageing law); inputs (HPPC pulse response versus ageing stresses and time); data scope (HPPC sequences of 104 cycle cells versus storage plus cycling conditions); split (random-looking cell split, 89/15, versus held-out conditions); sample-size axis (none versus m and k grid); function class (CNN-Transformer versus KAN); symbolic extraction (none); comparator (conventional Joint-L2 identification versus GAM, GP, LightGBM, Stroebl-type law).
- **Possible methodological contact to keep in mind:** their test cells are drawn from the same temperature groups as training, so their "unseen cell" claim is weaker than held-out-condition forecasting; this supports, but does not itself prove, why our split is stricter. Do not make that comparison in print beyond what Section 3.1 states.

## Hidden assumptions

- Conventional 1RC identification is an adequate reference for "reliability" of NN parameters (both fitted to the same voltage; agreement shows consistency of two fits, not physical accuracy; the authors say the Mean-CV reduction does not imply physical accuracy, line 447).
- Test cells are independent of training cells in ageing history (cell split only, selection of the 15 not described).
- Concatenated pulse segments with reset polarisation state represent a diagnostic sequence (line 384).
- OCV(SOC) and SOC are known at each step; their source is not described in the captured text.
