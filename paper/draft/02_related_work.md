# 2. Related work

## 2.1 Data-driven health and life models, including KAN variants

Data-driven models map features of a charge segment or of early cycles to state of health (SOH) or life. Examples are the cycle-life model of Severson et al. [@severson2019] and BatLiNet, which predicts lifetime across ageing conditions [@zhang2025batlinet]. Kolmogorov-Arnold networks (KANs) replace the fixed node activations of a multilayer perceptron by learnable univariate splines on the edges, so each input has a readable response function [@liu2024kan]. In batteries, KANs have been combined with recurrent, transformer and meta-learning models for SOH estimation, including papers in this journal [@jarraya2025; @yuan2026timexer; @wang2026meta; @fan2025]. Another study feeds them incremental-capacity features [@peng2024]. <!-- src: titles verified in refs_verified.md; full text read only for jarraya2025 (preprint), peng2024, liu2024kan; fan2025 is title only -->

## 2.2 Physics-informed SOH estimation

Wang et al. [@wang2024] proposed a solution network $F$ that predicts SOH, a second network $G$ for the unknown dynamics, and a loss term for the residual of $\partial_t F = G$, in the manner of physics-informed neural networks [@raissi2019pinn]. Physics-informed SOH estimation has since appeared in this journal as well [@chen2025pinn]. Several papers combine the idea with KANs. PIKAN and PGKAN, both in this journal, pair a KAN estimator with a dynamics module (a KAN in PIKAN, an adaptive-PDE surrogate in PGKAN) and report four public data sets [@pikan2026; @pgkan2026]. PI-EKAN uses eight data sets with a partial-charge window chosen per chemistry [@piekan2026]. A KAN with a Verhulst-type residual takes discharge curves of the TRI and XJTU cells as input [@liu2025verhulst]. SOH-KPINN is described by its title as a wavelet-enhanced physics-informed KAN [@sohkpinn2026] [TO VERIFY: title only]. These works estimate present SOH. As far as the sources read show, none forecasts the later capacity of a cell from its stress condition. For PIKAN this follows from the released code, which takes charge-segment features and no temperature, rate or depth-of-discharge input [TO VERIFY: PGKAN, PI-EKAN and SOH-KPINN full texts].

## 2.3 Physics-informed ageing laws and early-life forecasting

Models that place the stress dependence of ageing in the equation are closer to ours. Gasper et al. identify battery life models from data with machine learning: one paper treats calendar ageing by bi-level optimisation and symbolic regression [@gasper2021], and one treats cycling with bootstrap uncertainty estimates [@gasper2022]. Kuzhiyil et al. place a neural network inside the state equation of calendar ageing [@kuzhiyil2025]. PiDDM [@piddm2026] integrates an Arrhenius time term and an Arrhenius term in depth of discharge and throughput, with parameters supplied by a small network. It uses no storage cells, no state of charge and no KAN, and its test cells share protocols with the training cells. Guo et al. [@guo2026] forecast from the first 2 to 20 % of an unseen cell's record under a physics constraint (preprint). KA-CRNN, also in this journal, uses KAN-valued Arrhenius parameters that depend on state of charge, but for thermal-abuse kinetics measured by calorimetry [@kacrnn2026]. <!-- src: notes/papers/PRIORART_SUMMARY.md and priorart_*.md -->

On the data used here, the dataset authors fit a semi-empirical superposition of calendar and cycle loss [@stroebl2024], use it to design experiments [@palm2022], and report in this journal that its cycle part was inadequate [@stroebl2026]. A physical model combined with data under varying conditions has also been published in this journal [@ren2024] [TO VERIFY: title only]. Zhang et al. [@zhang2026] cite the dataset in a physics-informed model of equivalent-circuit parameters [TO VERIFY: full text unread; task and cells used unknown].

## 2.4 The gap

Physics-informed SOH networks estimate health from features. Stress-law models learn the dependence for one ageing mode, fix its form, or are not scored on held-out stress conditions of cells with storage and cycling records. We therefore test a combination whose parts each have precedent. It has one law with an Arrhenius calendar term whose coefficients are KAN functions of state of charge, a throughput-driven cycling term whose stress dependence is a KAN, and a soft physics residual. It is trained jointly on the storage and cycling cells of one dataset and scored on held-out cycling conditions from the first check-ups. Section 5 isolates each part by a prespecified comparison.
