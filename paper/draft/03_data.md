# 3. Data

## 3.1 Samsung INR21700-50E, the primary data set

The primary data are the multi-stage ageing study of Stroebl et al. [@stroebl2024]: 279 commercial cells of one type (rated 4.9 Ah) tested in two stages and three laboratories (HM, INT, SIE) with one protocol. Stage 2 used a new cell batch, so stage is a batch effect. <!-- src: notes/papers/samsung50e_dataset_paper.md; runs/prereg/PREREG_v4.md 3.1 -->

*Storage arm.* The arm has 132 cells. The data paper lists 22 test points, and our count from the metadata is 19 distinct conditions after repeated centre points are merged (10, 23 and 45 C; state of charge 5 to 100 %, limited to 80 % in stage 2 because stage 1 behaved non-monotonically above it). Only cells 01 to 03 of a test point (66 cells) have intermediate check-ups; cells 04 to 06 are excluded from training and scoring in the primary analysis. No storage condition exists at 35 C.

*Cycling arm.* The arm has 147 cells. The data paper lists 49 test points (71 with storage, as in [@stroebl2026]); our count from the metadata is 40 distinct conditions, with three cells at 35 conditions and 6, 6, 9, 9 and 12 cells at the five repeated centre points. Cycling temperatures are 23 C (10 conditions, 33 cells), 35 C (13 conditions, 39 cells) and 45 C (17 conditions, 75 cells). The design varies upper state of charge, depth of discharge (DoD) and charge and discharge rates within a 20 to 80 % window.

*Capacity and time.* The study provides no capacity table. Capacity at a check-up is the mean of the charge and discharge capacity (charge at C/2 to 4.2 V, discharge at 1C to 2.5 V, ambient 23 C), integrated from the logged current as in the data paper. Retention is capacity divided by a per-cell reference $Q_{0,c}$, the capacity of the 23 C entry test (ET_T23) of the cell, which comes from the same charge-and-discharge protocol as every check-up; whether an ET_T23 capacity exists for every analysed cell is decided once, before the outlier and incident steps, and is written to the run record. <!-- src: PREREG_v4 3.3(2) --> Retention is anchored on the ET_T23 capacity of each cell; the order of the entry tests inside ET (10, 45 and 23 C) was not verified, and if the 23 C test follows the others every cell carries the same one-time offset, absorbed identically into $Q_{0,c}$ for all cells and arms. <!-- C17: PREREG_v4 3.3(10)(g) --> Time is counted in calendar days since the reference; chamber and laboratory pauses stay on the real calendar axis. Throughput in equivalent full cycles (EFC) comes from the design rates. The analytic value is accepted if its median absolute relative difference from the logged throughput is at most 5 % and no sampled cell differs by more than 10 % at its last check-up; otherwise the logged value is used for every cycling cell [RESULT: median and maximum relative difference, and the branch taken]. <!-- src: PREREG_v4 5 (throughput validation rule) -->

*Quality control and exclusions.* A check-up, not its cell, is dropped when a prespecified flag fires (thresholds in Appendix A). The outlier rule is causal. A check-up is compared only with the two valid check-ups before it, and it is flagged when its capacity exceeds both of them by more than 1 % or lies below both of them by more than a time-scaled allowance (three times the latest fall rate times the elapsed days, plus 1 %). The first check-up is never tested and no later check-up enters, so window membership never depends on a post-window label and accelerating fade is not removed. <!-- src: PREREG_v4 3.3(3) --> Files that the data paper lists as faulty are dropped, for stage 1 only where the list names that stage; no cell is excluded for its capacity trajectory. A cell is keyed by serial and stage, and a cell whose stage checks disagree is excluded and listed [RESULT: cells excluded]. For 49 cells of one laboratory the serial inside the metadata differs from the design table; condition and stage of these cells follow the file names, the Testpoint field, laboratory and dates, which agree for all 279 cells. <!-- C17: PREREG_v4 3.2 and 3.3(10)(f) -->

*Disclosures on the development of the rules.* Capacities of 19 cells (18 cycling and 1 storage; 163 check-ups) were parsed and quality-checked before the preregistration was frozen, without scoring any model. ISU and SNL were explored in a scratch pilot before their splits were fixed; their results are therefore secondary and not blind. <!-- C17: PREREG_v4 3.3(10)(a) --> The outlier rule was developed on regular check-up (CU) rows; in the final data the 23 C entry-test (ET_T23) and extended check-up (exCU) rows enter its series, and the thresholds were not changed after the full release. <!-- C17: PREREG_v4 3.3(10)(e) --> The quality-control thresholds were fixed after those 163 check-ups, which belong to 19 cells (18 cycling and 1 storage), had been parsed, and the outlier rule was revised twice after the earlier forms had been inspected on them; a sensitivity row retains every check-up (Section 5.6). <!-- src: PREREG_v4 3.3(3) and 3.3(11)(a) --> A few capacity values of five cells were displayed during pipeline development, before the analysis plan was frozen, and informed no model or tuning decision; those of them that belong to test conditions were excluded from scoring. <!-- C17: PREREG_v4 3.3(11)(c) -->

*Confounds.* All 39 cells at 35 C were run by laboratory INT. Cycling at 45 C is HM (66 cells) and SIE (9 cells), and storage at 10 C is entirely SIE and at 45 C entirely HM. Temperature is therefore aliased with laboratory in the 35 C test of Section 5 and in part of the storage analysis. The capacity test is also not isothermal: the cell surface averages 31.6 C (stage 1) and 29.8 C (stage 2) during the 1C discharge in the 23 C chamber, and laboratories differ. The primary models do not correct for either effect; laboratory and stage are sensitivity rows. <!-- src: runs/samsung_pipeline/RESULT.md item 7; PREREG_v4 3.3(7) -->
