Multi-step prediction of battery state of health based on self-supervised
pre-training and transfer learning using the xPatch model

Zhu Yuan a,c, Zhongwei Deng a,* , Yvxin He a, Zhansheng Ning b,** , Jincheng Liu c

ARTICLE INFO              ABSTRACT

Keywords:                 Battery state of health (SOH) is a critical parameter in battery management systems, as its accurate prediction
Lithium-ion battery       ensures safe operation and efficient energy management. However, batteries experience complex nonlinear aging
State of health           processes, and variations in materials or usage conditions lead to diverse degradation modes, limiting prediction
xPatch model              accuracy. Moreover, single-step SOH prediction often fails to meet multi-scenario energy management re
Self-supervised learning  quirements. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a multi-step SOH prediction method for lithium-ion
Transfer learning         batteries (LIBs) based on self-supervised transfer learning with the xPatch model. The xPatch model combines
                          convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and multi-layer perceptrons (MLPs) as its backbone, while a dual-stream
                          mechanism captures long-term degradation trends and cyclic characteristics, balancing accuracy and computa
                          tional efficiency. To adapt to different degradation patterns, a self-supervised learning (SSL) strategy integrating
                          reconstruction, denoising, and prediction is designed. This framework not only reconstructs degradation be
                          haviors but also enhances robustness against noise, improving generalization across LIB types and conditions.
                          The proposed method is validated on three LIB aging datasets. Experimental results show an average SOH R2 of
                          0.964, while transfer learning improved R2 by 2.1 % compared to direct training, confirming the effectiveness of
                          the approach in achieving accurate and robust SOH forecasting.
