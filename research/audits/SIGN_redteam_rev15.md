The approved sentence says "[X] % lower" and a bracket "filled from `runs/`" but 9.8 and section 11 never say which analysis supplies X, Y, Z, W when the state comes from a row. The same gap holds for a row-lowered K2 (Z) and for the interval of a P0 reached by row downgrade. "Exactly one sentence set" holds; "a true sentence" does not.

**F3 (MAJOR, sections 1 and 11). P3 changes two things at once and the approved clauses name only one.** P3 = D_c = L_c(g1) - L_c(g4) with g1 = (m 8, k 2) and g4 = (m 32, k_full = 4) (PREREG 2, 9.4; `stats_v4.py:309-315`). Clauses e1, e1' and e2 say "the advantage was larger with 8 than with 32 cycling conditions" or "a dependence of the advantage on the number of cycling conditions was not shown". The contrast also changes the window from 2 to 4 check-ups, hence the forecast set (points after check-up 2 against points after check-up 4) and, for the capped cells (D032), the scored cell set (T45|0.8|0.6|1|2 goes from 4 to 7 scoreable cells at k = 4). D\* is re-selected per cell. Cheapest alternative explanation of a P3 pass: the data-driven learners, which read window features directly, gain more from two extra check-ups than A1 does (competing explanation E7 of section 1), and the grid cell (32, 2) that would isolate m is not in the test. Nothing in section 1 or 9 states this confound. The lead-in "eight cycling conditions and two check-ups" does not cure it.

**F4 (MAJOR, section 1). No gated claim carries an absolute skill criterion, yet the titles say "forecast".** T1-T3 and T9 ("forecast lithium-ion capacity fade from few cycling tests and early check-ups") are earned by P1 tier S, which means a median per-condition error ratio above 1.02 against the inner-CV winner of {A9, A11, A12} at one cell. This is the cheapest explanation of a positive P1: the comparators at m = 8 are weak (two inner folds, near-trivial data-only fits), not that A1 forecasts usefully.

- T9a/T9b say "Physics priors pay when cycling tests are few" with M2 (P+ needs no P3), while e2 says "a dependence of the advantage on the number of cycling conditions was not shown".

The state K+ tests only accuracy (K1 within 10 % of A1, K2 better than G15). 11a item 10 and 7.3 item 6 decline to name any form or direction of any extracted effect, temperature is suppressed, and the stability table has no threshold and is "descriptive". - D\* includes A9 (`selectors.py:12`, "the data-driven learner" may be the PINN network without the law). When D\* = A9 in a fold, P1 and P2 are the same contrast on those conditions and the "most" test is implied; the simulation of chain probabilities (9.3, RES) assumed separate arms.

**F6 (MAJOR, section 10). The NC-1 pass criterion can false-pass a NaN.** `negcontrol.py:76-79`: `diffs` holds `np.max(np.abs(a-b))` per cell; `mx = max(diffs)` and `passed = (mx == 0.0)`. Python `max` ignores a NaN that is not the first element. Reproduced with the same expressions: `diffs = [0.0, nan]` gives `mx = 0.0` and `passed = True`. A leaking arm that returns NaN under the scrambled labels (clipping to 0.0 makes `log(ret)` -inf) passes Gate G if its cell is not first. `summarise` (`negcontrol.py:85`) has the same `max`. `test_pk_negcontrol.py` has no NaN case. The gate that defines "no title and no claim" is thereby blind to the failure mode a scramble to the clip boundary produces.

**F7 (MAJOR, section 10). The stated coverage of NC-1 exceeds what the code and the evidence deliver.**
- 10 "Grid coverage" says NC-1 runs "for every (outer fold, m) tuning record". In a confirmatory run (`run.py:518`, `:560-567`) only the predict-stage check exists; tuning, selection, margins and the D\*/AG choice are compared only by `nc1_full`, which is synthetic-only (`run.py:297-300`), one fold, m in {8, 32}. m = 16 and four of five folds of tuning are never invariance-checked.
- The real-data build path (`build_data` with `keep`, the ET anchoring, blinding exclusions, ZYK throughput, the D025 cap, `lab_map`) is not exercised by any invariance run, and the synthetic cells contain no short-record cells.
- The only evidence on file, `runs/freeze_shakedown/RESULT.md`, was produced at 42c2a84 and states it does not count; FB-9 and C16 are open, so no NC-1 evidence exists at the commit that would be frozen. The signature can cover the design and the code only.

**F8 (MAJOR, section 10). NC-2 cannot fail, so its wording consequence is not earned.** Criterion: median(L) > 0 at margin 0 (30 of 40) for L = ln(S_A1,permuted / S_A1,real). `metrics.py:190` replaces each permuted cell's logged throughput by `spec.efc_rate(donor raw) * t`, including inside the window, so the permuted run also loses the cell's own measured operating rate (the quantity the window and the causal tail use).
