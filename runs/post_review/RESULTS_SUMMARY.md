## Item 6 - discrete grid for the state factor h(y)

Grid (8 members): constant; self-limiting 1/(1 + y/Y0) with Y0 in {4, 2, 1, 0.5}; accelerating exp(gamma y) with gamma in {0.15, 0.3, 0.6} (y in the model's y_s units). Applied with the frozen context manager law.overrides. A2 (law only, h binds fully) is refit with its tuned hyperparameters for all 8 members; A1 (tuned hyperparameters, tuned lambda) for the members that differ from the fold's frozen backbone (the member equal to the fold's frozen backbone is A1 as frozen). 'Inner-fold choice' = the frozen backbone tournament (A2 at default settings on the inner folds of the training conditions, simplest member within one SE of the best) extended to the 8 members, simplicity order constant, Y0 4, 2, 1, 0.5, gamma 0.15, 0.3, 0.6; the held-out score of the chosen member is read from the grid runs. This is a discrete choice from the explored grid, not a learned h.

Score = condition RMSE in percentage points (the frozen primary endpoint: cell RMSE over the forecast set, averaged within condition; ensemble of the five seeds unless stated). g1 = m 8 training conditions scored at k = 2; g4 = m 32 scored at k = full; 40 held-out conditions over the five outer folds. 'ln ratio' = ln(variant / reference) per condition, negative = the variant has the lower error; better/worse/tie = number of conditions with ln ratio below / above / equal to 0.
