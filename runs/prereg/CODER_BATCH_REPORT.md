## C12 pp-scale margins
`margins.pooled_margins(run_dirs)` (also `python -m pinnkan.margins --runs d1,... --out runs/prereg/margins.json`): E = comparator's training-side inner-CV score per outer fold from `margins_inputs.json`, pooled by test-condition count; delta_S = 0.02 E, delta_N = 0.10 E, plus the 9.2 sensitivity margins; D* and AG use the arm selected in each fold (`comparators.json`) with its own E. Formed per contrast (`A1 vs D*, A9, AG, A2, A10, A10P, A13`, `A15 vs A1`, `A15 vs G15`) and per grid cell `m<m>_k<k>` (the inner-CV score is computed once per (fold, m), so the k cells of an m share E), plus a `named` map (P1, P2, K1, K2, secondary). Refuses: a comparator present in only some folds, missing folds, duplicate (m, fold), a selector score that disagrees with E, `margins_inputs.json` newer than a condition-scores file (strict). Non-finite E gives no margin, recorded. Wired into the statistics step: writes `margins.json` in the analysis directory, adds `pp_margin` and `tier_pp` (BCa lower bound of the mean pp gain against delta_S and -delta_N) to every estimated row and `named_tier_pp` to `out["margins_pp"]`; runs without `margins_inputs.json` give "not available", a mixture raises. No margin value is chosen by hand.
