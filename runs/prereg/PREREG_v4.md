## 1. Claim contract

- **Question (J16 1.1).** How few cycling ageing tests and early check-ups does a physics-informed neural network (PINN) with Kolmogorov-Arnold (KAN) stress functions need to forecast held-out cycling conditions of the Samsung INR21700-50E campaign? How much of any advantage comes from the physics law and how much from the KAN? Does the KAN yield a closed-form stress law that predicts held-out conditions at least as well as an equally simplified additive law? The word "few" applies to **cycling** conditions only: the 132 storage cells stay in training in every grid cell, so titles say "few cycling tests", never "few ageing tests".
- **Claims, each with its own gated test (sections 2 and 9).** *PINN role, chain P:* **P1** at the scarce cell g1 = (8 cycling conditions, 2 check-ups) the PINN-KAN (A1) has a lower median held-out-condition error than the best tuned data-driven learner D\*; **P2** the same network without the physics law (A9) is worse at g1; **P3** the advantage over D\* is larger at g1 than at the full-data cell g4. *KAN role, chain K:* **K1** at g4 the closed-form law extracted from the KAN (A15) is non-inferior to A1; **K2** at g4 A15 is more accurate than an equally simplified additive law (G15). *Qualifiers (secondary family):* Q-H (A1 against the hard ODE A2), Q-A10 (A1 against the dataset authors' model) and Q-T (training with and without storage cells). Nothing else is a claim.
- **Estimand (D012; J16 section 2).** Unit = held-out condition (Test B, n = 40). For a contrast with target arm T and comparator arm C at grid cell g, L_c = ln(S_C,c / S_T,c) with S the condition score (section 6); L_c > 0 favours T. The test is the **exact one-sided sign test** on L_c - m; the estimand is the **median condition**. Conventions for the abstract numbers (fixed here because V3 and T1 carry a sign slip in "1 - exp(median L)"; the code uses the form below): a relative error reduction is X = 100 (1 - exp(-median L)) %; an excess "is Y % worse" is Y = 100 (exp(median L) - 1) % with L taken in the direction that makes it positive; both come with the exact order-statistic 95 % interval transformed the same way. The mean-based pp difference with a BCa interval and the worst-condition error are descriptive companions (section 9.8 rule 1).
- **Mechanism.** (1) The soft residual holds a shared surface F to a **single state-dependent modulation** of the sum of a time-driven and a throughput-driven rate where there are no data (beyond the window, between conditions, at 35 C storage twins). (2) A hidden-layer KAN stress map represents interactions of (T, SOC window, DoD, C_ch, C_dch) without prespecifying them; hidden width h = 1 is in the grid, so at m = 8 the map can collapse towards a near-additive form. (3) The hypothesis behind P3 is that a physics prior pays when cycling conditions are scarce and matters less as they grow. The law is never called "independent superposition" (D013 (a)).
- **Closest methods and exact distinction.** Stroebl et al. (dataset authors; parametric calendar + cycle superposition fitted to this data; no learned stress maps; A10) and their JES 142, 119552 (states the cycle part is inadequate) **[FB-2]**. PiDDM (arXiv 2607.29095; fixed law, parameters from a small MLP of stress; no KAN, no joint storage-cycling training, no held-out-condition protocol as far as the re-audit read). KA-CRNN (JES 158, 121853; KAN-valued Arrhenius parameters of SOC in a neural ODE on thermal-abuse data). PIKAN/PGKAN (SOH estimation, PINN4SOH sets, per-cell life-fraction scaling; not forecasting). Zhang JES 182, 125097 (equivalent-circuit parameter evolution with a PINN; cites this dataset) **[FB-1]**: positioning unchecked until read. Guo et al. (arXiv 2607.16864; early-life prediction from 2-20 % of life, on file): the **data-efficiency framing** of this paper must be re-checked against it and against Zhang, Hallinger and Stroebl JES 142 by the novelty auditor before any "to our knowledge" sentence (J16 5.4); until then the word "first" is not used.
- **Competing explanations of an advantage of A1 and the control for each.** E1 gain from the per-cell latent z_c and window inversion, not physics or KAN: A9 has the same z_c, inputs and inversion (P2); NC-2 (section 10) tests whether stress dependence is needed. E2 gain from capacity or tuning budget: equal trials per arm at every grid cell, grids extended at pilot edges, parameter counts and `n_train_used` reported. E3 gain from lab/stage/temperature aliasing and non-isothermal check-ups (3.3(7)): lab-stratified and lab-and-stage-indicator rows including A10 and D\* (9.9), with an automatic tier downgrade. E4 gain from label-free design-hull information the data-only learners never see (collocation in the training hull, calendar twins): part of the mechanism and a stated limitation; the D\* feature option with the PINN's transformed inputs narrows it. E5 gain specific to the window, horizon or the training set an arm can use: k = 0 reference, k = 2 against k_full, `n_train_used` row. E6 gain from the 132 storage cells (cross-regime information), not from the law: Q-T (A13). E7 gain from the check-ups rather than from the conditions: the window-information gain (k = 2 against k = 0) is estimated for every arm. E8 an apparent KAN-law advantage that is only fewer parameters: coefficient counts and the training-side BIC of A15 and G15 are printed beside K2. E9 selection of a favourable subset of 8 conditions: nested label-free subsets and the random-subset row at g1 (downgrade rule).
- **Expected endpoint (J16 1.5 and section 7; the judge's reading of the files, not a result).** K1 is likely to pass (non-inferiority power near 0.96 at rho = 0.95, SR section 7); K2 is likely N or none (pilot P4 found a forecast gap of 0.0-0.1 pp between KAN and GAM); Q-H is likely none (pilot P5, synthetic ISU design: median soft/hard ratio 0.96 with the judge-specified collocation and 0.84 with whole-axis collocation, which is now a tuned categorical; J16 quotes the 4 % gain; RT F12.7); Q-A10 at g4 is likely S (D011) and at g1 unknown and possibly reversed; **P1 is the open risk: no measurement exists for g1.** At full data the best data-driven learners already sit near the replicate spread (pilot P3), so no full-data win over them is expected. The realistic best case is title T2; an equally plausible case is T5, an honest learning-curve paper that is weaker for JES. Shlok was told this (status file section 00, written 2026-11-05 04:05) and approved the table (D017, 07:50). The ISU pilot P6 gives the first signal before the freeze (17.1, J16 5.6).
- **Power (T1/SR; assumptions, not Samsung numbers).** Exact sign test, n = 40, tier S, at the level a/3 tabulated in T1 (P1's initial local level is a/2 = 0.0125, where T1 gives the same 28 of 40 positive conditions; the simulation of item (b) re-evaluates it): 80 % power detects a median relative improvement of about 9-12 % / 12-16 % / 17-22 % when the two arms' condition errors correlate 0.95 / 0.90 / 0.75 (T1); non-inferiority at true equality (margin 10 %): 0.71-0.95 / 0.40-0.72 / 0.16-0.32 (T1). P1 compares different model families, so rho = 0.75 is the working assumption and the detectable gain at g1 is 17-22 % (J16 1.5). Power of P1 at g1 for rho in {0.6, 0.75, 0.9} at local level 0.0125: [SIM: item b]. Chain probabilities for P2 and P3 given P1: [SIM: item b]. Power of K1 and K2: [SIM: item c]. Error-correlation estimates from inner-CV condition scores (ISU in pilot P6, Samsung training sides; power report only, no rule changes): [SIM: item f]. Test C (n = 13) cannot reach 80 % below 20 % and cannot support a non-inferiority claim: it is descriptive.
- **Abstract rules.** The abstract sentence, title and qualifier sentences are those of the closed outcome table (section 11) and nothing else; their numbers come from `runs/` through the statistics step. Words never used: those listed at the end of section 11. Scope words: "held-out cycling conditions of one cell type in three laboratories". No p-values, no "significant".

### 3.3 Inclusion and exclusion (rules; J11 C6, C8)
1. Capacity per check-up = mean of charge (step 21) and discharge (step 22) capacity by trapezoid integration of the current (pipeline `q_mean`). A check-up failing a critical flag below is dropped, not its cell.
2. Reference Q0,c: ET_T23 capacity if ET members exist for every analysed cell, otherwise the first quality-passed CU for every analysed cell (one reference for all; by availability) **[FB-3]**. The current pipeline output is anchored on the first CU (fade before it is unmeasured). Retention = Q / Q0,c.
3. **Pipeline QC flags (critical; set qc_pass false) as implemented in commit 3415db2:** step 21 or 22 missing; step durations out of range (22: 0.45-1.35 h; 21: 0.9-2.8 h); median current off nominal; sampling gap > 60 s inside the step; NaN current; capacity outside 2.0-5.2 Ah; discharge not from >= 4.0 V to <= 2.55 V; charge not to 4.2 V;
   |q_ch - q_dch| / q_dch > 3 %; cell surface temperature at the start of the discharge more than 5 C from 23 C; setpoint not 23 C; start date missing; start dates decreasing with file number; and the **causal isolated-outlier rule** (V3E 5; review m1): with a, b the two preceding valid check-ups, r = max(q_a - q_b, 0) / (days between them) the latest fall rate
   and dt the days since b, check-up i is flagged when **q_i exceeds max(q_a, q_b) by more than 1 % of q_i (rise)**, or when **q_i is below min(q_a, q_b) - 3 x r x dt - 1 % of q_i (fall beyond a time-scaled allowance of three times the latest fall rate)**. ## 5. Task, window rule, forecast set

- **Task (identical for every arm).** Inputs: stress vector s (cycling: T, SOC_mid, DoD, C_ch, C_dch; storage: T, SOC), Q0,c, window check-ups, throughput rate nu. Output: retention at every later check-up time. Time t_tilde = days since the reference check-up (t_n = days / 500), global scaling only.
- **Throughput.** Inside the window: logged (analytic `efc_est_cum` of the pipeline) equivalent full cycles; after the window: the cell's own mean rate over the window (causal). **Validation rule (fixed now) [FB-5]:** analytic EFC versus ZYK-logged EFC for at least 2 cycling cells per DoD class and per laboratory, at every check-up. Accept analytic throughput iff the median absolute relative
  difference is <= 5 % and no sampled cell exceeds 10 % at its last check-up; otherwise ZYK-logged throughput is used for every cycling cell (`throughput: zyk` is a stub that raises until implemented, so this branch needs code before the freeze, 17.4). An oracle-throughput row is a sensitivity either way.
- **Window rule in check-up counts, from the schedule alone [FB-6].** Count intermediate check-ups (CU + exCU; ET and AT excluded) per cell from the zip member listings (identifiers only). n_med = median over cycling cells; **k_cyc = round(n_med / 3)**, where `round` is Python's (halves go to the even integer; 3.5 gives 4), as implemented in `window_rule_from_counts`; likewise k_sto from storage cells 01-03.
  Primary window = first k check-ups after the reference, k in {0, 2, k_full} with **k_full = k_cyc** (section 4.1); the V3 secondary windows k_full - 1 and k_full + 2 survive as sensitivity rows at g4 only (9.9). Storage cells in training use min(k, k_sto) window points when a learner needs a window summary. Expectation (GA N1): the two listed pilot-P0 cycling cells have 8 and 15 intermediate check-ups and the storage cell 7; k_cyc is expected in 3 to 5, k_sto near 2; the code defaults k = 4 and 2 are placeholders until counted. **k_full is counted from member listings before any label is read; k_full < 2 re-opens the design (J16 section 8).**
- **Schedule go/no-go (J§8-3).** If fewer than 3 post-window check-ups exist for most cycling cells at the primary window, Samsung becomes secondary (storage transfer only) and the fallback of 3.4 applies.
- **Forecast set.** For each scored cell: all CU and exCU after the window surviving 3.3, in time order. A cell is scored with at least one such check-up; the number of points per cell is reported; a sensitivity row restricts to cells with >= 3 forecast points.

## 6. Endpoints

- **Primary endpoint (one; judge's endpoint, pp):** cell-level retention RMSE (percentage points) over the forecast set, averaged within condition (mean over the condition's scored cells) then over conditions, at the grid cell in question; Test B held-out conditions, 5 seeds (per-condition value = mean over seeds of that condition's RMSE), frozen configuration. **Every primary and secondary contrast is the sign test on the log ratio of condition scores** (9.3); the pp difference is reported with every tier. Condition scores exist for each of the nine cells and each arm that runs at it.
- **Secondary:** the other windows of 5; error at fixed horizons after the window; RUL error in days for cells crossing 80 % (n reported); MAE, MAPE, R^2 (correct argument order); Test C, S2; wall clock and parameter count (coefficient counts for A15 and G15); share of non-finite or diverging forecasts per arm (a non-finite forecast is scored with the persistence error and counted); `n_train_used` per arm and cell; `E/R_LOO` and `E/R_WARP` (descriptive, 9.10). The code writes R^2, MAPE, error by horizon and RUL error into the outer-test `metrics.json`.
- **Metrics where a regression is expected:** wall clock and parameter count; error at the first post-window check-up (a data-only learner can be locally better); RUL error (few cells reach 80 %); the worst-condition error of A1 (a median estimand does not penalise it; 9.8 rule 1).

## 7. Arms, tuning, budget (J16 1.2-1.3, section 3; D013; code README)

**Common law and recipe (D013 (a), (b), (d); README "Decisions made while building").** Law: dy/dt = [r_cal(T, SOC) + r_cyc(s) nu(t)] h(y), y_s = 10 (1 - retention), t_n = days / 500, E_n = EFC / 500. **One state factor h per fold shared by the time-driven and the throughput-driven term** ("single state-dependent modulation"; this gives the closed-form integral
y = Psi^-1(a_cal t_n + a_cyc E_n(t)), checked against RK4); it is chosen once per outer fold by a tournament on training conditions with the hard-ODE-KAN at default settings among {constant, self-limiting 1/(1 + y/Y0), accelerating exp(gamma y)} (Y0 = 1, gamma = 0.3), the simplest within one standard error of the best, shared by all law-based arms of that fold.
The per-cell offset delta_c scales the term(s) the cell has (the cycling term for a cycling cell, the calendar term for a storage cell), prior sd TAU = 0.15 fixed. Calendar KAN [1,2] from storage SOC to (a, E) is identical in every law-based arm; the calendar term of a cycling cell uses (T, SOC_mid); calendar twins are (T, SOC_mid) of the training cycling conditions (+ the 35 C twins in Test C). Cyclic KAN over
(T, SOC_mid, DoD, C_ch, C_dch); own compact cubic B-spline KAN (`code/models/kan_spline.py`); KAN sparsity (L1 + entropy) is computed on edge parameter magnitudes (a proxy for the activation-based penalty of the KAN literature). Stress inputs are scaled to [-1, 1] with fixed physical ranges (T 5-50 C, SOC 0-1, DoD 0-0.8, C_ch 0-1.2, C_dch 0-2.2) and clamped inside the spline grid.
Solution network F: tanh MLP width 32, depth 3, per-cell latent z_c of dimension 2, hard initial condition; fixed in every F-based arm and not tuned (conservative for the claim; A9 gets a larger space).
**Optimiser recipe (fixed constants of `law.py`, accepted as D013 (d)):** Adam, learning rate 5e-3 with cosine decay, **global gradient-norm clip 20 in every gradient-trained arm (A1, A2, A4, A5, A9, A13, A15 and every other gradient-trained arm)**, residual weight ramped from 0 to lam_res over the first 40 % of the steps in arms with a residual. The recipe was fixed after a divergence on a synthetic design (clip off: data MSE 1.7 and 34 at lam_res 0.1 and 1);
the clipped-step fraction is logged. Collocation sets: (a) record end or whole axis (tuned categorical, below), (b) Latin-hypercube points in the training hull, (c) calendar twins; causal test-time inversion (z_c, delta_c from the window plus the residual over the forecast horizon).
**Where the recipe is a choice, not a result:** the A9/A2 clip, the 40 % warm-up and the 1e-3 grid point were set before any Samsung score; sensitivity rows (k) vary clip {10, 50} and warm-up {0.2, 0.6} for P2 at g1 and g4.

**Budget rule (J16 1.2).** Every tuned arm gets the same N trials (TPE, sampler seed = outer-fold index) at every (outer fold, m), with the inner folds of 4.1 (2 for m in {8, 16}, 3 for m = 32; grouped by condition, stratified by temperature), inside outer-training conditions only, plus 5 final refits (seeds 0-4) per arm and cell that is scored; failed trials are redrawn until N valid ones exist (more than max(2, N/4) failures raise).
N = 20; N = 40 if the median fit on a Samsung training fold is under 20 s for every neural arm (J§6). The budget is fixed **before tuning** by one timing fit per neural arm (`auto_budget`, `budget_rule.json`). **Timing run at the freeze commit [FB-8] (D008(a), J11 C3):** if the median hard-ODE fit exceeds 30 s or the PINN fit exceeds 90 s, the J§7 Partial plan applies to **every arm equally** (trials 20 -> 10, inner folds at m = 32 from 3 to 2, seeds stay 5). Edge-optimum rule: a value at a grid edge extends the grid in the next version.

### 7.3 The closed-form KAN law (A15) and its matched GAM law (G15) (J16 section 3)

1. **Frozen symbolic library and extraction rule,** committed with a hash before any outer prediction, identical for A15 and G15. Library per edge: zero (pruned); linear; quadratic; power x^p on a positive physical abscissa; log; exponential; Arrhenius exp(-b/T) on temperature only; hinge max(0, x - c). Each surviving edge is fitted on a fixed grid over the fold's training hull and chosen by BIC. **A per-edge cap on the number of free coefficients is [ARCH: cap]** (set by the method-architect before the freeze; the same for both arms). The free coefficients are then refitted end to end under the same loss.
2. **Matched GAM simplification (G15).** Same library, BIC rule, cap and refit, applied to A4's additive terms. The calendar KAN [1,2] is the same in both arms and is extracted identically, so K2 isolates the cyclic map, where the KAN's composition layer (the part an additive GAM lacks) can matter. Coefficient counts and training-side BIC of both laws are printed beside K2.
3. **External test = prediction of held-out conditions.** K1 and K2 are scored on Test B outer conditions that the extraction never saw.
4. **Unit tests (freeze condition 17.2).** A noiseless synthetic law is recovered; with an untruncated library A15 reproduces A1 to float tolerance; G15 and A15 coincide when h = 1 and the outer edge is linear.
5. If the extraction cannot pass its unit tests by the freeze, chain K is struck before the freeze, its weight of 1/2 goes to chain P (P1 tested at 0.025), the K state is fixed at K= with the K1-fail clause of the table, and the table is otherwise unchanged (J16 section 8).

## 9. Statistical analysis (D012; J16 section 2; SR sections 2-4; T1)

### 9.1 Unit
**Unit = held-out condition** (Test B 40, Test C 13, S2 19, ISU 63, SNL 23); equal weight per condition (the five centre conditions are not up-weighted). Paired by condition (both arms scored on the same cells and windows, and, across the grid, on the same held-out conditions). Neighbour dependence, fold-shared fits and the nested-subset correlation across grid cells are not modelled in the test (stated); their effect on the size is simulated, [SIM: item a]; per-fold medians and the number of folds with sign agreement are reported.

### 9.2 Margins
*Log scale (primary):* L_c = ln(S_C,c / S_T,c); superiority margin ln(1.02) (tier S), non-inferiority margin -ln(1.10) (tier N). *pp scale (descriptive beside every tier):* delta_S = 0.02 E, delta_N = 0.10 E, E = the comparator's training-side inner-CV condition-mean score at that grid cell, per outer fold, pooled by test-condition count; `runs/prereg/margins.json` (E, deltas, hash, per contrast and cell) is written **after tuning ends and before any final refit is applied to a test condition**. Sensitivity rows: delta_S in {0, 0.05}, delta_N in {0.05, 0.15} (relative). The replicate spread is in no margin or gate.

### 9.3 Primary test (D012; the sign-flip rule is retired)
One-sided **exact sign test** of median(L - m) > 0, i.e. the number of conditions with L_c > m must reach a count k, binomial(n, 1/2), no RNG; conditions with L_c exactly equal to m are dropped and n reduced. Tier S passes m = ln 1.02; tier N passes m = -ln 1.10 but not S; "none" otherwise; "reverse tier S" passes the mirrored test (the comparator is better by the S margin, count of L_c < -ln 1.02). Reported with the exact order-statistic 95 % interval of the median (coverage >= 0.95) and the transformed relative figures of section 1. The Wilcoxon and sign-flip rules are appendix-only.
Positives needed at n = 40 (T1 exact binomial): **28 at level 0.0125 (actual size 0.0083), 27 at level 0.025 (size 0.0192)**, 29 at level 0.00625 (size 0.0032); counts for other levels and for n < 40 (blinding exclusions, 3.3(11)) come from the same exact rule in the statistics step.
**Type-I verification.** The sign test holds its size for any continuous distribution with independent conditions (T1, FB-7 closed). Conditions share folds, labs and, across grid cells, nested training subsets (RT F4). ### 9.4 Primary graph (gated; family-wise error rate 0.025 one-sided)

## 10. Controls (D014; NC sections 3 and 6; J16 1.3)

1. **Close the blockers** of 17.1 and collect the signatures of 17.2; confirm that the working tree is clean under `code/` and `runs/prereg/` (`git status --porcelain --untracked-files=no -- code runs/prereg` empty).

2b. **Additional pins for Design R2.** `runs/prereg/subsets.json` (nested subset ids per outer fold, hash), `runs/prereg/blinding_exclusions.json` (3.3(11)), the frozen symbolic library file with its hash and the value of `[ARCH: cap]` (7.3), the statistician's decision files of `runs/prereg/stats_sim_v4/` (section 21) with their hashes, and `k_full`/`k_sto`/N or the Partial plan from steps FB-6 and FB-8. 3. **Final text.** Produce the frozen prereg file: strike every [FB-n], [D], [SIM: ...], [ARCH: ...] and [GAP-n] marker, fill the values (k_cyc, k_sto, N or Partial plan, A10 equation numbers, seeds), set the status line to FROZEN, commit it. The freeze commit is the commit that contains the final prereg text, the splits, the configs and the code. (`PREREG_DEFAULT` in `guard.py` names `runs/prereg/PREREG_v3.md` since commit ca66522; it is changed to the final file (section 20) or `build_freeze_payload` is called with `prereg_file` set to the final file, e.g. `runs/prereg/PREREG_v4.md` or its frozen successor, and FROZEN stores that name.)
4. **Who writes FROZEN.** **The lead** (not the experiment designer, not the statistician, not any code): after the signatures. Nothing in the package writes the file. The lead calls `guard.build_freeze_payload(root, split_keys, config_paths, prereg_file)` with `split_keys = [("samsung","B"), ("samsung","C"), ("samsung","S2"), ("samsung","A"), ("isu","B"), ("snl","B")]` (plus A/S3 entries as run) and the list of confirmatory config paths, and writes the returned dict as JSON to `runs/prereg/FROZEN`.
5. **FROZEN fields** (from `build_freeze_payload`): `commit` (git rev-parse HEAD at the call), `prereg_file`, `prereg_sha256`, `splits` = {"<dataset>:<test>": {`file_sha256`, `fold_hash`}}, `config_sha256s` (canonical hashes of DEFAULTS merged with each config file). The file is committed in the same step only if the commit is the frozen commit itself; the simplest order is: commit everything, call the payload builder, write FROZEN as an untracked flag file (the guard checks tracked files only), run `verify_freeze`. HEAD must then not move until all confirmatory runs finish (a later commit invalidates the freeze, by design).
6. **What the guard then enforces.** `verify_freeze`: FROZEN present; prereg hash equals the pin; HEAD = commit; tracked files under `code/` and `runs/prereg/` clean. `load_split(role="test")` and the scorer call it; the split file and fold-hash pins are checked on every load (training role too); a confirmatory real-data run needs its canonical config hash in `config_sha256s`, `steps_scale = 1`, no `overwrite`, and is appended to
   `runs/prereg/SCORED.jsonl` (a second scoring of the same config is refused; a folder holding `condition_scores.json` is never replaced). Samsung un-blinds only on a valid freeze. At run start `DeviceError` is raised if CUDA is visible.
7. **After scoring.** Run the statistics step (`code/run_pinnkan_stats.py`) on the fold runs; the figure scripts read its outputs. Any rule change after a Samsung test fold is scored is a new version with a change-log row and a disclosure (stop rule 7).
